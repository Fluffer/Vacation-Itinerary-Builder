## Money & Practical Info
- Currency: Vietnamese Dong (VND). Rate ~ 18,500–19,000 VND = 1 SGD (Nov 2025; volatile — recheck via XE before travel)
- Cash: prefer VND for street food, markets, taxis. Cards accepted at hotels, malls, mid+ restaurants
- ATM: BIDV, Vietcombank give best rates; avoid yellow Euronet (poor rates, high fees)
- Tipping: not mandatory but 10% appreciated at sit-down restaurants and spas (50k–100k VND per spa session)
- Power: 220V, 50Hz, Type A/C/F sockets (NOT Type G UK-style). SG/UK plugs do NOT fit — bring universal travel adapter. Most 4★+ hotels have universal sockets but don't rely on it.
- Emergency: Police 113, Ambulance 115, Fire 114
- Philippine Embassy Hanoi: +84 24 3943 7948 / consular hotline; PH Consulate has no Da Nang office — Hanoi handles Central VN
- Singapore exit/re-entry: confirm SG Long-Term Pass / Work Pass / Re-entry permit valid through 18 Aug 2026 if SG-resident
- Tap water: NOT drinkable; bottled water only (most hotels supply free daily)
- Weather Aug (Da Nang specifically): transitional — historically warm/mostly dry (27–34°C, 75–85% humidity) BUT August now sees rising afternoon thunderstorm frequency and edge-of-season tropical disturbance risk. Pack light cotton + SPF50 + packable rain shell + quick-dry shoes. Monitor Vietnam Met (nchmf.gov.vn) and PAGASA 1 week before flight for any tropical system tracking near Central VN.

## Risk Flags & Mitigations
1. **Dragon Bridge show**: Confirmed Fri/Sat/Sun at 21:00 — Aug 14 = Fri ✅
2. **Saturday Pub Crawl on Aug 15** ✅ matches itinerary
3. **Day 3 packed**: Marble + Hoi An back-to-back is long (NOT leisure pace). Skip Marble if hungover from crawl, OR move Marble to Day 4 morning swap with Son Tra. Allow 5+ hours in Hoi An for unhurried wandering.
3b. **Day 2 over-booked**: Full-day Ba Na PLUS late pub crawl = high fatigue. Realistic options: (a) take Ba Na half-day, return 13:00, rest 3hr; (b) skip pub crawl; (c) downgrade Day 3 pace to compensate.
4. **Day 5 departure buffer**: REVISED to 3hr (depart hotel 15:00, airport 15:30). Original 2.5hr too tight per Scoot recommendation.
5. **Aug weather**: dry season tail; possible afternoon thunderstorms; have Plan B (Fantasy Park indoor at Ba Na, café-hop in Hoi An)
6. **Typhoon risk**: Central VN typhoon season starts Sep — Aug 14–18 generally safe but monitor PAGASA/VN Met
7. **Lady Buddha dress code**: covered shoulders & knees — bring light scarf/long pants
8. **Marble Mountains slip risk**: limestone, sometimes wet — proper shoes
9. **Hoi An lantern boat**: negotiate price BEFORE boarding (50k–100k VND fair)
10. **Single-hotel base**: confirmed optimal for 5-day Da Nang trip
